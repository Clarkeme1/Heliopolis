A day in Heliopolis

Suggested itinerary for a day in Heliopolis

Heliopolis is a Northeastern district of Cairo, Egypt, known for its historical significance and modern urban development. Originally known as Iunu and renamed Heliopolis (or "City of the Sun") by the Greeks, it served as Egypt's premier spiritual hub for sun god worship. In 1905, Belgian industrialist Baron Édouard Empain reinvented the area as a modern, upscale garden suburb featuring a distinct blend of European, Moorish, and Hindu-inspired architecture. Today, its unique legacy thrives through iconic landmarks like the arcaded Korba district and the beautifully restored Baron Empain Palace.

Heliopolis is also known in Arabic as Masr El Gedida, meaning "New Egypt".

More About Heliopolis

After living in Heliopolis for over two months, I wanted to share my personal itinerary for how I would spend a day there. In Egypt, walking and just watching what happens on the street is half the fun so I tried to make this custom route walkable, even though there are some longer stretches.

I like to start the day off by seeing a few historic landmarks, like the palace and the cathedral. There are several cafe's and places to grab a quick pastry and cup of coffee which you can't miss! Then I enjoy talking with locals in the park. After, I made my way to Korba square where you can go shopping in all the colorful shops and markets. It is a great way to spend the evening watching locals while grabbing a delicious bite to eat from all the available street vendors.
